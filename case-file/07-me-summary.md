## RANGE OF FIRE

Examination of both entrance wounds found **no soot and no stippling (no gunpowder
tattooing)** on the skin or clothing margins. These findings are **inconsistent with a
contact or near-contact shot** and indicate the muzzle was beyond close range —
generally **greater than approximately 3–4 feet** from the body at the time of
discharge.

## FORENSIC INTERPRETATION (limited)

- The **upward, front-to-back trajectories** are consistent with the decedent's torso
  being **inclined or leaning forward** toward the shooter at the moment of discharge
  (for example, lunging or advancing). They are **less consistent** with an upright
  individual standing still or stepping backward.
- The **absence of soot/stippling** is consistent with a distance of more than a few
  feet between muzzle and decedent.
- These are physical findings only. Determinations regarding intent, threat, or
  justification are outside the scope of this report.
