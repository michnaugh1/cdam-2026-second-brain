## SYNOPSIS

On the above date and time, deputies responded to a 911 report of shots fired at
Ramona's Sane Bar & Grill. Upon arrival, deputies located an adult male (later
identified as **Marcus HALE**, DOB 11/02/1996) deceased from apparent gunshot
wounds inside the establishment. A second adult male, **Daniel REYES**
(DOB 06/22/1991), remained on scene, was detained without incident, and stated he
had fired in self-defense. A handgun, a closed folding knife, and two spent
casings were recovered. The Medical Examiner and Det. Salas were notified.

## NARRATIVE

Dep. Boyd arrived at 2359 hrs, approximately 4 minutes after dispatch. The
establishment is a bar and grill; staff advised the kitchen had closed and only a
small number of patrons remained. The main room was dimly lit. I observed that the
overhead light fixture above the pool table area on the east side of the room was
**not operating**; staff stated the bulb had been out "for a couple days." Lighting
in that area was low.

The decedent (HALE) was located supine in the east-center of the room, between the
support column and the pool table. A **closed folding knife** was on the floor
approximately 2 feet from the decedent's right hand. Two **9 mm spent casings** were
located approximately 10–11 feet from the decedent's position, near the base of the
support column.

REYES was seated near the bar and immediately identified himself as the person who
fired. He stated, in substance, that HALE had come at him and reached toward his
waist, that he believed HALE was armed, and that he fired to protect himself. REYES
produced a valid Michigan Concealed Pistol License. A semi-automatic pistol was
recovered from REYES and made safe.
